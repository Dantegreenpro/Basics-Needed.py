## 🎯 How to Use These Exercises

1. **Start with your level**: Begin with exercises matching your current skill level
2. **Read the problem carefully**: Understand what's being asked before coding
3. **Try without looking at solutions**: Challenge yourself first
4. **Test your solutions**: Run your code with different inputs
5. **Compare with provided solutions**: Learn from different approaches
6. **Optimize your code**: Think about efficiency and readability

## 💡 Tips for Success

- **Practice regularly**: Even 15-30 minutes daily helps
- **Don't give up easily**: Struggling is part of learning
- **Use debugging tools**: Print statements and debuggers are your friends
- **Ask for help**: When stuck, research or ask for guidance
- **Explain your solution**: Try to explain your code to someone else
